The Journey

Built from the Ground Up

⚡

The Pause That Didn't Stop Him

In his first year at Chitechma University, financial hardship forced Milton to pause his studies — no laptop, no shortcuts. Rather than surrender, he took on the roles of bar attendant and then manager at Figaro Bar, independently funding his return: his machine, his clothes, his future. He came back to campus not defeated — but forged. Stronger. Unstoppable.

🎯

The Spark — A Bootcamp, A Mentor

Introduced to the world of mobile development through an intensive bootcamp held at Chitechma University and HIBES University, Milton was mentored by Mr. Chia Desire — the renowned developer and visionary founder of HND Pathfinder. Flutter clicked. The framework became his weapon of choice, and he never looked back.

👂

Listening to Real Problems

Managing a business put Milton in direct contact with community friction — student struggles, local bottlenecks, youth challenges with no digital solutions. Where others saw complaints, he saw product briefs. Every grievance became a feature. Every pain point, a sprint. This is the philosophy embedded into every line of his code.

🎓

Where He Is Now

Currently completing his final semester exams for Level 1 at Chitechma University, Buea. An internship awaits, followed by HND examinations next year. The arc is clear. The trajectory is steep. The determination is absolute.
